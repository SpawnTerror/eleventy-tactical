# Privacy &amp; disclaimer

## Privacy and cookies

`example.com` is a static site. It does **not** set tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics scripts.

Your browser's `localStorage` is used solely to remember your optional visual preferences (light/dark theme, T-Side/CT-Side colour scheme, and CRT toggle). That data never leaves your device.

If you email us (for example via the contact or scammer report links), your email address and message are used only to respond to your enquiry or review your submission.
